🪜The path

Level 1

Fundamentals

DC and AC circuits, phasors, power, three-phase systems. Getting started with NRJXpert.

 Level 2

Power networks

Lines, cables, transformers, per-unit, voltage drop, load flow (Gauss-Seidel, Newton-Raphson), IEC 60909 short circuit.

 Level 3

Renewable energy

Solar and wind resource, PV module, stand-alone and grid-connected PV sizing, wiring and costs, wind and the Weibull distribution, solar water pumping.

 Level 4

Economic studies

Discounting, life-cycle cost, LCOE, NPV, IRR, payback, minimum tariff and subsidy, sensitivity.

 Level 5

DER and smart grids

Distribution load flow, controlled inverters, hosting capacity, dynamics, inverter short circuit, optimisation.

 Level 6

Smart grids: architecture, operation, owner's engineering

SGAM, SCADA/ADMS/DERMS, self-healing and reliability, IVVC/r, VPP, microgrids, e-mobility, CIM and IEC 61850, IEC 62559 use cases, expert skills.

 Level 7

Expert workshop

Microgrid controller test bench (IEEE 2030.8), smart metering and non-technical losses (DLMS/COSEM, G3-PLC), OpenADR 2.0b demand response, IEC 62443 / IEC 62351 cybersecurity.

 Consulting

Case studies

Village electrification, grid-connected PV plant, industrial substation, coastal wind, solar water pumping: from the data to the design note.

📗How to work

  1. Read the theory of each section: definitions, assumptions, equations. The blue boxes recall the formulas to remember.
  2. Do the example by hand (calculator, spreadsheet) before looking at the solution: that is where understanding happens.
  3. Check with NRJXpert in the « Lab » boxes 🛠: draw the diagram or enter the data, run the calculation and compare with the hand calculation. A difference must always be explained (assumption, rounding, model).
  4. Practise with the solved exercises (« Show the solution ») and the calculators 🧮, then answer the quiz at the end of the level.
  5. Vary the data: what happens if the length doubles, if the voltage goes from 24 to 48 V, if the discount rate changes? Habit makes the engineer.
Example files. The projects quoted in the tutorial are in the folder ~/.nrjxpert/aide/tutoriel/exemples/, created when the tutorial is first opened. In NRJXpert, Help › Open a tutorial example… opens each file in the right window: network diagrams .nxp, photovoltaic projects .nxpv, wind projects .nxeol, solar pumping projects .nxpomp. The .xml files are networks as the client sends them to the calculation server: they open in the Calculate a network file (XML) window, where values can be changed before running.
Calculation server. Network calculations (levels 1, 2, 5, 6 and 7, case studies 2 and 3) are performed by the NRJXpert calculation server, built into the software and running on your computer (default setting « Use the local server on this computer », Simulation › Calculation and server settings): you only need to activate your licence (Help › License…). All the levels can then be worked offline; the Internet is only used for licence checks and online weather data.

🧩Detailed contents

Level 1 — Fundamentals

  1. Quantities, Ohm's and Kirchhoff's laws
  2. Sinusoidal steady state, impedances, power
  3. Reactive power compensation
  4. Three-phase systems
  5. Getting started with NRJXpert

Level 2 — Power networks

  1. Line and cable models
  2. Transformers
  3. Per-unit system
  4. Voltage drop and losses
  5. Load flow: formulation
  6. Gauss-Seidel, Newton-Raphson, fast decoupled
  7. Short circuit: IEC 60909, symmetrical components
  8. Protection: breaking capacity, selectivity

Level 3 — Renewable energy

  1. Solar resource
  2. The photovoltaic module
  3. Stand-alone PV sizing
  4. Wiring: cross-sections, protection, costs
  5. Grid-connected PV
  6. Wind resource and Weibull distribution
  7. Wind production and sizing
  8. Solar water pumping

Level 4 — Economic studies

  1. Time value of money
  2. Life-cycle and discounted cost
  3. LCOE
  4. NPV, IRR, payback
  5. Minimum tariff and subsidy
  6. Sensitivity and comparison of options

Level 5 — DER and smart grids

  1. Load flow of distribution networks
  2. Controlled inverters and hosting capacity
  3. Time series
  4. Microgrid dynamics
  5. Inverter short circuit
  6. Economic dispatch and OPF
  7. Optimal hybrid sizing

Level 6 — Smart grids: architecture, operation, owner's engineering

  1. SGAM architecture (IEC SRD 63200)
  2. OT/IT operation: SCADA, ADMS, DERMS
  3. Self-healing (FLISR) and reliability
  4. Volt/VAR control (IVVC, IVVC/r, CVR)
  5. Virtual power plants
  6. Microgrids and smart inverters
  7. E-mobility (V1G, V2G)
  8. Data models and protocols
  9. Owner's engineering and project governance
  10. Skills matrix

Level 7 — Expert workshop

  1. Microgrid controller tests (IEEE 2030.7 / 2030.8)
  2. Smart metering: technical and non-technical losses, fraud
  3. DLMS/COSEM data collection, G3-PLC
  4. OpenADR 2.0b demand response
  5. IEC 62443 / IEC 62351 cybersecurity
  6. Expert assignment

Complements

  • Four consulting case studies
  • Teacher's guide: progressions, lab sessions, marking schemes, subjects
  • Appendices: formulas, cable tables, c factors, constants
